Accessibility Statement

Everyone deserves a clear path to help.

We want this website to work for everyone, including people who use assistive technology.

Last reviewed: September 2026

Our commitment

New Beginnings Recovery is committed to making newbeginningsrecovery.com accessible to the widest possible audience, regardless of ability or technology. We are actively working to increase the accessibility and usability of our website.

Conformance target

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA, the standard referenced by the Americans with Disabilities Act and adopted by the U.S. Department of Justice’s web accessibility guidance. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible for people with a wide range of disabilities, including visual, auditory, physical, speech, cognitive, and neurological conditions.

What we do

  • Provide text alternatives for meaningful images.
  • Support keyboard navigation with a visible focus indicator and a “skip to main content” link.
  • Maintain sufficient color contrast for text and interface elements.
  • Use clear headings, labels, and landmarks so pages are easy to navigate with a screen reader.
  • Respect “reduce motion” system settings.

We do not use an accessibility “overlay” widget. Overlays do not reliably fix accessibility problems, so we build accessibility into the site itself.

Known limitations

This site is being actively rebuilt, and some pages may not yet fully meet our target. We are prioritizing fixes and welcome your feedback. If you encounter a barrier, please tell us — we will work to provide the information or service you need through an accessible alternative.

Contact us about accessibility

If any part of this website is difficult to use, call us at (760) 762-3605. Please describe the problem, the page, and the assistive technology you use so we can help and improve.

In an emergency, call 911. If you or someone else is in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), available 24/7. This website is not a substitute for emergency medical care.
