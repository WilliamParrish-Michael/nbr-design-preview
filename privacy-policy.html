Privacy Policy

Your privacy, in plain terms

Draft for legal review. This page is built to the compliance requirements identified in the site audit and must be reviewed and approved by New Beginnings Recovery’s attorney before the site goes live.

Effective date: [to be set at launch]

This Privacy Policy explains how New Beginnings Recovery (“we,” “us”) collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you visit newbeginningsrecovery.com (the “Site”). Health information you provide as a patient is also protected under our Notice of Privacy Practices and, for substance-use-disorder records, under federal law (42 C.F.R. Part 2). Where those apply, they govern that information.

Information we collect

  • Identifiers & contact data — name, phone, email, and similar details you submit.
  • Insurance information — provider and member details you share to verify benefits.
  • Health / substance-use information — details you choose to share about treatment needs. This is sensitive personal information.
  • Internet & usage data — pages viewed and similar analytics, collected only with your consent (see Your Privacy Choices).
  • Inferences drawn from the above to help us respond to your inquiry.

How we use information

To respond to inquiries, verify insurance, provide and improve our services, communicate with you (with your consent), maintain security, and comply with law.

How we share information — and your opt-out

We do not sell your personal information for money. However, when analytics or advertising technologies are enabled with your consent, sharing certain identifiers with those partners for cross-context behavioral advertising may be considered a “sale” or “share” under California law. You can opt out at any time through Your Privacy Choices, and we honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signals as a valid opt-out. We do not use advertising or analytics trackers on pages where you submit health information.

Sensitive personal information

We limit our use of sensitive personal information (including health and substance-use information) to what is necessary to provide the services you request. See “Limit the Use of My Sensitive Personal Information” under Your Privacy Choices.

Your California privacy rights

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know, access, correct, and delete your personal information; to opt out of sale/sharing; to limit the use of sensitive personal information; and to not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

How to exercise your rights: submit a request by (1) calling (760) 762-3605 or (2) emailing info@newbeginningsrecovery.com. We will verify your identity, respond within 45 days (extendable as allowed by law), and offer an appeal if we decline. An authorized agent may submit a request on your behalf with proof of authorization.

Cookies & tracking

Non-essential cookies and trackers load only after you opt in, and we honor GPC. Manage your choices any time via Your Privacy Choices.

Consumer health data

For consumer health data covered by state consumer-health-data laws, see our Consumer Health Data Notice.

Children

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Data retention & security

We retain personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described or as required by law, and we maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards.

Changes & contact

We may update this policy and will revise the effective date above. Questions? Call (760) 762-3605 or email info@newbeginningsrecovery.com. 34620 Via Josefina, Rancho Mirage, CA 92270.
