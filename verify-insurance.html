Insurance

Find out if your insurance covers treatment

A quick, confidential benefits check — no obligation. Most major insurance plans are accepted, and we never share your information.

How verification works

  1. 1

    Call or reach out

    A quick, confidential conversation with our admissions team — no obligation.

  2. 2

    We check your benefits

    We contact your insurer directly to confirm what your plan covers for detox and residential treatment.

  3. 3

    We explain your options

    Clear information on coverage, any out-of-pocket costs, and flexible payment options if you’re self-pay.

What to have ready

  • The name of your insurance provider
  • Your member ID (from your insurance card)
  • A good phone number where we can reach you

Your privacy comes first. For anything involving health or insurance details, please call us directly rather than sending them by email. We handle your information confidentially and never sell or share it for advertising.
