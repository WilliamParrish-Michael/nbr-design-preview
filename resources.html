If you or someone you love is struggling, you’re not alone. Start with the support lines below, then reach out when you’re ready.

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

Call or text 988, 24/7, free and confidential.

SAMHSA National Helpline

1-800-662-HELP (4357) — free, confidential, 24/7 treatment referral and information.

Emergency

If someone is in immediate danger, call 911.
