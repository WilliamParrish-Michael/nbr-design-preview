Our Commitment

Nondiscrimination & accessibility

Draft for legal review. This page is built to the compliance requirements identified in the site audit and must be reviewed and approved by New Beginnings Recovery’s attorney before the site goes live.

Effective date: [to be set at launch]

New Beginnings Recovery welcomes and serves everyone. We do not discriminate against any person on the basis of race, color, national origin, ancestry, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, marital status, age, disability, medical condition, source of payment, or any other characteristic protected by California’s Unruh Civil Rights Act and other applicable law.

[New Beginnings Recovery is a private-pay facility and does not participate in Medi-Cal, Medicare, or other federal programs, so the federal Section 1557 notice-and-tagline requirements do not apply. This page is a voluntary statement of our commitment. If the facility later accepts any state or federal insurance or grant funding, a full Section 1557 Notice of Nondiscrimination and a Notice of Availability of Language Assistance Services (with taglines in the state’s top 15 languages) would become legally required.]

Help for people with disabilities

If you have a disability and need a communication aid or service to access our care — for example, a qualified sign-language interpreter, or written information in large print, audio, or another accessible format — please tell us. We will work with you to communicate effectively, at no cost to you. Call (760) 762-3605 (TTY: 711) or email info@newbeginningsrecovery.com.

Language help

If English is not your preferred language, let us know and we will do our best to help you communicate with our team. [Confirm the facility’s actual language capabilities before launch. Given the Coachella Valley’s large Spanish-speaking community, offering Spanish support — bilingual staff or a phone interpreter line — is strongly recommended, and this section should state honestly what is actually available.]

Questions or concerns

If you have a concern about discrimination or access, contact us at (760) 762-3605 or info@newbeginningsrecovery.com. 34620 Via Josefina, Rancho Mirage, CA 92270. You may also have rights under the Americans with Disabilities Act and California law.

In an emergency, call 911. If you or someone else is in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), available 24/7. This website is not a substitute for emergency medical care.
