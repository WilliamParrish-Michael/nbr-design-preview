Residential Rehab

A safe place for healing

Residential rehab is more than a program — it’s a chance to step away from the chaos of addiction and begin again in a safe, structured, and compassionate environment.

What is residential rehab?

Residential (inpatient) rehab is a structured program where clients live on-site during recovery. It provides 24/7 support and supervision, a safe space away from triggers and daily stressors, a structured daily routine that builds stability, and access to therapies, wellness services, and community support.

A day in residential rehab

Morning

Individual or group therapy, guided reflection, or wellness activities.

Afternoon

Skill-building, relapse-prevention groups, or family sessions.

Evening

Community meals, mindfulness practices, or quiet time to recharge.

Therapies offered

  • Individual counseling
  • Group therapy
  • Family therapy
  • Trauma-informed care
  • Relapse-prevention training

FAQs about residential rehab

What happens in residential rehab?

Clients live on-site and participate in a structured schedule of therapy, wellness, and recovery activities. Meals, housing, and 24/7 support are included.

How long is inpatient rehab?

Most programs last 30–90 days, depending on personal needs, progress, and goals.

Is residential rehab effective?

Research shows inpatient rehab can significantly improve recovery outcomes compared to outpatient care alone, especially for those with severe addictions.

What should I bring to rehab?

Comfortable clothing, personal hygiene items, and any necessary medications. Our admissions team provides a full packing list.

Is residential rehab covered by insurance?

Many major insurance plans are accepted. Our admissions team can verify benefits and walk you through coverage options.
