Serving the Coachella Valley

New Beginnings is a trusted local option for safe, compassionate addiction treatment serving Rancho Mirage, Palm Springs, Cathedral City, Indio, and Riverside County. Our programs address alcohol, opioids, prescription medications, and multiple substances — always with dignity and respect.

Frequently asked questions

What types of addiction treatment programs do you offer?

Medically supervised detox, residential treatment, dual diagnosis support, and personalized therapy plans.

How do I know which program is right for me?

Our admissions specialists conduct confidential assessments to determine the appropriate level of care based on substance use history, medical needs, and treatment goals.

Are your programs covered by insurance?

Many major insurance plans are accepted. Our team can help verify your coverage and explore payment options.

What should I expect during treatment?

Structured therapy, medical care, wellness activities, and skill-building in a supportive environment designed to stabilize health and develop tools for long-term sobriety.
